# World and Story

## Setting

The game takes place in the fictional late-1800s **San Perdido Territory**. Its main settlement, **Mercy's Wake**, grew where frontier roads, river trade, and a Gulf Coast port meet.

Poker never became part of local culture. Mahjong is the only formal table game most residents know, and its outcomes carry social and legal weight. Disputes involving status, debts, land, influence, and access to information are commonly settled at the table.

## Regions

1. **Wayward Farm** — Doc's inherited farmstead outside town
2. **Bridlewood Ranch** — neighboring homestead, pasture, animals, and horse progression
3. **Dustward** — rough frontier street, saloon culture, general store, and working residents
4. **Saint's Landing** — wealthy European-influenced port neighborhood
5. **Ironhook Docks** — cargo, warehouses, traders, and commercial waterfront
6. **Gull's Rest** — coastal fishing village, fish market, cabins, and quiet shore
7. **Red Testament** — desert routes leading to Texas King's secret compound, King's Reach

Dustward and Saint's Landing are distinct neighborhoods connected by a restricted gate or bridge. The restriction begins as a property dispute controlled by Texas King.

## The hall

The original **Six Brands Hall** stands in Saint's Landing. Texas King owns it and operates it as a private club. Doc gradually wins access to sections of the same building and restores it through five visible stages:

1. Restricted and privately controlled
2. Cleanup and structural access
3. Functional practice room
4. Public tournament hall
5. Legendary restoration

Players can freely place optional decorations while major restoration milestones use authored states.

## Opening

Doc inherits Wayward Farm from his missing Uncle Silas. The farm begins modest but functional:

- Furnished farmhouse
- Eight usable crop plots
- Basic storage
- Small barn
- Repairable animal pen
- Stable or hitching post
- Pond or well
- Two locked expansion areas
- Decoration zone
- Shipping crate

Silas seemed uninvolved with town power, but Doc gradually discovers that his uncle had been investigating Texas King and the supernatural bargain behind the Six Brands.

## Texas King

Texas King is an ageless outlaw, wealthy landowner, and legendary player who made a supernatural bargain. Townspeople generally believe he is merely unbeatable. Some admire the money and stability he brought, while others distrust his control.

The bargain prevents Texas King from aging and protects land, status, and wealth won under the legitimate Six Brands rules. Anyone who accepts his table is spiritually bound to honor the result. The bargain can be broken only when a blood descendant masters all six Brands, defeats him under the hall's legitimate rules, and wins the hall back.

Texas King knows from the beginning that Doc is his son. Doc learns the truth only at the very end.

## Uncle Silas

Silas discovered Texas King's identity and the bargain while investigating him. Texas King trapped Silas through the bargain before he could warn Doc. Silas survives, returns for the ending, and remains part of the postgame.

## Opponent structure

The eleven launch opponents form flexible tiers:

- Three beginners
- Four established townspeople
- Three experts
- Texas King

Players may challenge available opponents in any order within an unlocked tier. Every opponent has:

- Fixed primary and secondary Brands
- Fixed skill level and recognizable table personality
- Repeatable matches and wager pools
- One substantial multi-stage storyline
- A first-victory reward
- Active helper options and permanent passive benefits

Final names mix historically grounded names with Western nicknames. Comedic names may conceal serious personal stories.

## Finale

The restored hall hosts the championship. Doc learns and counters every altered rule Texas King uses; the game never resolves the finale through hidden random cheating.

After losing the match, Texas King draws his weapon. Dialogue choices determine available evidence and support, followed by a short timing interaction. Doc directly shoots and physically kills him. The scene includes some pixel blood but no gore. Failure restarts immediately before the standoff, not before the Mahjong match.

Texas King's death reveals that he was Doc's father.

## Endings

1. **A Town Reclaimed** — property disputes and major character stories are resolved
2. **Keeper of the Six Brands** — the hall, records, and supernatural history are completed
3. **The Good Earth** — farm, ranch, fishing, and community life define Doc's future
4. **A Hollow Victory** — Texas King dies, but insufficient preparation leaves the town divided

A clear warning and separate autosave occur before the final tournament. The postgame continues after every main ending with Texas King dead, Silas returned, unfinished stories available, advanced Mahjong unlocked, and King's Reach open for exploration.
