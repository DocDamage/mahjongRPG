# Game Vision

## Identity

**Six Brands at High Noon** is an open-ended cozy Western narrative RPG. It combines a compact, memorable settlement structure with farming, ranching, fishing, relationships, exploration, property disputes, and a novel one-on-one Mahjong game.

The first release intentionally excludes conventional combat. Doc's gun animations support story tension and the final standoff, while progression comes from mastery, access, relationships, tools, property, and restoration.

## Tone

The game balances:

- Dry and dark comedy
- Serious Western drama
- Cozy daily routines
- Relaxed exploration
- Supernatural mystery
- A small amount of non-gory pixel blood in the finale

## Protagonist

Doc is a 35-year-old man with a fixed identity and personality. He is dry, dark, sarcastic, funny, and friendly once people know him. He begins alone but may support additional playable characters in a later expansion.

## Primary goals

- Defeat all eleven recognized Mahjong opponents
- Master and unlock all six Brands
- Restore the legendary Six Brands Hall
- Recover disputed properties and public access
- Develop Wayward Farm
- Build relationships through multi-stage character stories
- Complete farming, animal, fishing, recipe, and Mahjong records
- Discover what happened to Doc's missing uncle
- Defeat Texas King and continue in an open-ended postgame

## RPG progression

The initial game does not use character levels, HP, MP, armor, or random battles. Progression comes from:

- Mahjong rank and Deeds
- Brand upgrades and loadouts
- Tools and fishing equipment
- Property deeds and story access
- Farm buildings and free placement
- Animal breeding and named lineages
- Cooking and processing
- NPC active help and permanent bonuses
- Dialogue decisions and ending state
- Collections and records

## Platforms and presentation

- Windows first
- Godot 4.7.1
- Top-down pixel art
- Four-direction player movement
- Keyboard and modern controller support
- 960×540 internal resolution
- Integer-scaled output
- One continuous world assembled from scrolling maps, fixed-screen maps, and short transitions according to source-asset construction

## Launch boundaries

Deferred systems include:

- Conventional combat
- Party combat and additional playable characters
- Boat fishing and water navigation
- Full mounted Doc animation
- Multiple seasons
- Additional settlements beyond the launch territory

These are expansion paths, not hidden launch requirements.
