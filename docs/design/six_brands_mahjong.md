# Six Brands Mahjong

## Purpose

Six Brands Mahjong is a new one-on-one Western variant designed for full 15–25 minute NPC matches. It keeps the readable core of drawing, discarding, sequences, matching sets, pairs, hand reading, and risk management while replacing traditional scoring language with Western terminology.

## Tile identities and Brands

The game uses the traditional core tile identities across six complete colorways:

- Blue — River
- Dark — Night
- Green — Homestead
- Orange — High Noon
- Pink — Hospitality
- Purple — Omen

Every tile has a normal Mahjong identity and a Brand color. Brand symbols and patterns accompany color so the rules remain readable without color perception.

All six colors appear in every match. Before a match, the player equips two unlocked Brands. Both remain locked for the full match. Each NPC has a fixed signature Brand and a fixed secondary Brand.

Doc begins with Orange and Blue.

## Progressive rules

### Trail Rules

Early opponents teach an 11-tile structure:

- Three groups and one pair
- Runs use three consecutive numbered tiles of one suit
- Sets use three identical tile identities
- Honors form pairs or matching sets
- Three copies of each traditional identity
- 102-tile wall
- Exactly 17 tiles from each Brand
- Quads excluded

### Frontier Rules

Advanced opponents and the restored hall use the full structure:

- Four groups and one pair
- Four copies of each traditional identity
- 136-tile wall
- Approximately 22–23 tiles per Brand
- Extra-color allocation rotates between hands
- Quads may be introduced

Completing a structurally valid hand is always enough to win. **Deeds** add Renown but are not mandatory for hand validity.

## Match structure

1. Morning Hand
2. High Noon Hand
3. Sundown Hand
4. Midnight Hand
5. Showdown Hand only when Renown is tied

The dealer alternates automatically. A completed match advances the world clock by 90 in-game minutes regardless of real reading speed.

## Brand charging

Discarding a tile that matches one of the player's equipped Brands adds one charge to that Brand. Three charges create one activation. Charges reset at the start of each hand.

Powers may be earned repeatedly, with a maximum of two stored activations per Brand. Every Brand eventually supports upgrade choices.

## Brand claims and abilities

### Blue — River

- Special claim: complete a Run from an opponent discard
- Active concept: recover one of the player's two latest discards, then discard another tile
- Identity: recovery, flexibility, and water flow

### Dark — Night

- Special claim: counter or deny an opponent claim
- Active concept: inspect a tile, lock a tile for one turn, make a discard unclaimable, or cancel a Brand power
- Identity: concealment, disruption, and control

### Green — Homestead

- Special claim: complete a matching Set
- Active concept: place one tile in the Corral, draw a replacement, and recover the stored tile later
- Identity: protection, reservation, and patient building

### Orange — High Noon

- Special claim: complete any group, while granting the opponent one Brand charge
- Active concept: draw two tiles, keep one, and return the other to the bottom of the wall
- Identity: speed, pressure, and aggressive declarations

### Pink — Hospitality

- Special claim: establish the hand's Pair
- Active concept: exchange through a neutral face-up space or clear a hostile effect
- Identity: support, trade, negotiation, and cleansing

### Purple — Omen

- Special claim: reserve a marked discard until the beginning of the next turn
- Active concept: inspect and reorder the next three wall tiles
- Identity: supernatural knowledge, planning, and foresight

Every player may claim a discard that directly completes a winning hand. Brand rules govern non-winning claims.

Direct interference may inspect a concealed tile, make a discard unclaimable, lock a tile temporarily, or cancel a power. The game does not rearrange an opponent's concealed hand or change the identity of an opponent tile.

## High Noon declaration

When one tile from victory, a player may declare High Noon:

- The hand locks completely
- Additional Brand powers are disabled
- A visible three-draw countdown begins
- Draws and discards resolve automatically while waiting
- Winning before expiration grants a major Renown bonus
- The opponent gains personality-specific counter options
- Failure ends the declaration but does not destroy the hand

The exact winning tiles remain hidden on standard and advanced assistance settings.

## Deeds and Renown

Traditional scoring terminology is replaced by Western names. Example Deeds include:

- Three Trails — three Runs
- The Posse — matching Set-focused hand
- Claimed Territory — one numerical suit
- Home Turf — strong concentration in the player's equipped Brands
- Cattle Rustler — heavy use of an opponent's Brand colors
- Full Frontier — all six Brands represented under a qualifying rule
- Quickdraw — win through a declared High Noon state
- Self-Made — win from the player's own draw
- Long Trail — complete 1–9 progression across groups
- Against the Odds — win after the opponent declares High Noon

Deeds are discovered gradually when opponents demonstrate or teach them. Results always explain each Renown award.

## Wagers and losses

Each match supports:

- Friendly
- Serious
- High Stakes

Wagers may use dollars, table tokens, produce, fish, recipes, lures, tools, furniture, information, property access, or story-specific deeds. Ordinary lost items return to the NPC's wager pool for immediate rematch. Unique losses remain recoverable through a rematch or a dedicated retrieval quest. Main progression is never permanently destroyed.

- Winning-tile and wait display

- One undo per turn

### Trailhand

### Gunslinger

- Minimal assistance
- No discard recommendations
- No automatic danger warnings
- Legal-action validation and final scoring remain visible

Mahjong assistance is independent from overall farming and fishing difficulty.

## AI fairness

- NPCs never inspect concealed tiles without a visible ability
- NPCs do not know wall order without a visible Purple effect
- Difficulty changes reasoning quality, memory, risk tolerance, and planning depth
- Hostile actions are announced and logged
- Matches use deterministic seeds
- Post-match history can reconstruct every draw, discard, claim, power, and score
- Texas King follows learnable supernatural rules rather than hidden outcome manipulation
